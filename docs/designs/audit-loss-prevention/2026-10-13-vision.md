# Vision: Giám sát & chống thất thoát

**Module:** 11 — Audit & Loss Prevention Service  
**Ngày:** 2026-10-13  
**Trạng thái:** Approved with warnings — user approved after Vision review  
**Scope assessment:** L — Full Ceremony  
**Parent architecture:** EventBridge + SQS, transactional outbox, database per service, Cognito RBAC, OpenTelemetry/X-Ray

## 1. What we're building

Audit & Loss Prevention là bounded context tập trung để tiếp nhận, lưu trữ và truy vấn các audit/security events từ các service POS. Audit service là nguồn lưu bằng chứng append-only; các service nghiệp vụ vẫn sở hữu business state và chỉ phát event sau khi mutation của mình commit thành công.

### P0 — Audit evidence

Ghi nhận đầy đủ, có thể truy vết và không thể sửa trực tiếp đối với:

- **Hủy món:** ai thực hiện, effective operator/cashier, order/line, chi nhánh, lý do, trạng thái trước/sau và kết quả.
- **Giảm giá/override:** actor, order/line, promotion hoặc manual override, số tiền trước/sau, lý do, quyền/phê duyệt nếu có và kết quả.
- **Mở két:** actor/effective cashier, shift, POS device, chi nhánh, nguồn trigger, thời điểm yêu cầu/kết quả và correlation trace.

Mọi event có tenant/branch scope, actor context, resource reference, timestamp, reason/outcome, correlation/causation và before/after summary đã redaction. Raw JWT, secret, PIN, payment credential, phone và payload không cần thiết không được lưu.

### P2 — Phát hiện bất thường hành vi nhân viên

Loss Prevention worker đọc các audit, order, payment và shift/cash facts để áp dụng rule-based guardrails và baseline thống kê theo nhân viên, chi nhánh và khung giờ. Hệ thống tạo `anomaly finding/case` có severity, evidence, rule version, confidence/explanation và trạng thái xử lý.

P2 chỉ tạo cảnh báo/case cho Owner hoặc Manager trong phạm vi được phép. Hệ thống không tự động khóa tài khoản, từ chối thanh toán hoặc chặn thao tác nghiệp vụ trong MVP.

## 2. Why

Các thao tác như hủy món, giảm giá và mở két có thể gây thất thoát hoặc tranh chấp nếu chỉ nhìn thấy trạng thái cuối trong Order/Payment/Shift. Chủ quán cần bằng chứng bất biến để điều tra theo người, chi nhánh, ca và thiết bị; quản lý cần thấy cảnh báo dễ giải thích thay vì một điểm số ML không thể kiểm chứng.

Thiết kế này tạo một audit trail thống nhất cho toàn platform, bảo toàn boundary database-per-service và tận dụng event-driven architecture. P0 ưu tiên tính đầy đủ, bất biến, tenant isolation và khả năng truy vết; P2 ưu tiên tín hiệu dễ giải thích, false-positive control và workflow điều tra.

## 4. Product experience

Audit console dùng server-resolved scope, không cho client tự mở rộng tenant/branch:

- Bộ lọc theo thời gian, branch, actor/effective operator, action, resource type, outcome, device, shift và correlation ID.
- Timeline event immutable với safe labels, reason, before/after summary, source service, event version và trace links.
- Trạng thái dữ liệu rõ ràng: `FRESH`, `STALE`, `PARTIAL`, `UNAVAILABLE`, `NO_DATA`; không biến thiếu event thành “không có vi phạm”.
- Anomaly queue có severity, finding state (`OPEN`, `ACKNOWLEDGED`, `INVESTIGATING`, `RESOLVED`, `DISMISSED`), evidence references, rule version, explanation và resolution note.
- Owner/Manager chỉ thấy actor/resource/evidence trong scope. Cashier/Kitchen không có quyền truy cập console trong MVP.

Audit UI là công cụ điều tra và kiểm soát; không chỉnh sửa event lịch sử. Thao tác acknowledge/resolve/dismiss là một mutation audit mới, có actor, reason và before/after trạng thái case.

## 5. Data flow and ownership

```text
Order / Payment / Shift / Identity / POS Device
        │ transactional outbox
        ▼
EventBridge → SQS audit queue → Audit consumer + inbox/dedupe
        │                              │
        │                              ├─ append-only audit_events
        │                              ├─ query projection/index
        │                              └─ watermark/DLQ/integrity metrics
        ▼
Loss Prevention worker
  ├─ rule guardrails
  ├─ branch/employee/time baseline
  └─ anomaly findings/cases + audit event
        │
        ▼
Audit API / Angular console / restricted Ops / safe downstream facts
```

Audit service owns audit evidence, query projection and anomaly cases. Order, Payment, Shift, Identity and POS Device remain owners of their own state. No service writes directly to Audit tables and Audit does not mutate source business state.

## 6. Success criteria

| Area | Outcome | Measurement direction |
|---|---|---|
| Completeness | P0 cancel/discount/open-drawer actions emit one durable audit fact after source commit | Contract fixtures, source/outbox reconciliation and no-loss replay test |
| Integrity | Historical audit events are append-only, deduplicated and tamper-evident at storage/operation boundary | Unique event/source keys, hash/chain or integrity evidence, restricted writes |
| Traceability | Investigator can connect actor, effective cashier, branch, shift, device, resource, reason and distributed trace | End-to-end correlation fixture across Order/Payment/Shift/POS |
| Authorization | Owner sees tenant scope; Manager cannot read another branch through filters, IDs, pagination or case references | Negative IDOR/branch/revocation tests |
| Privacy | Audit evidence contains only redacted/minimal data; no raw JWT/PIN/payment secret/customer phone | Schema, log/trace scan and security tests |
| Freshness | Recent events become queryable within an agreed operational lag and expose watermark/quality state | Queue/projection lag metrics and stale/partial UI tests |
| Explainability | P2 findings show rule version, evidence and reason; reviewers can acknowledge/resolve/dismiss | Golden anomaly fixtures and case workflow tests |
| Safety | P2 never autonomously blocks an employee or changes order/payment state | Architecture boundary and negative integration tests |
| Retention | Audit/security evidence retained at least 7 years, with legal hold and controlled archive/delete policy | Retention policy test and restricted operations audit |

## 7. Scope boundaries

### In scope

- Central append-only Audit service and redacted audit/security event envelope.
- P0 events for cancel item, discount/override and cash drawer open.
- Audit query API/console with tenant/branch RBAC and immutable timeline.
- Audit access/replay/DLQ/integrity monitoring.
- P2 rule-based anomaly findings using audit + approved operational facts.
- Case lifecycle and audit trail for acknowledge/investigate/resolve/dismiss.
- Seven-year minimum evidence retention, legal hold reference and restricted operations.

### Out of scope

- Automatic blocking, suspension, payroll action or disciplinary decision.
- ML anomaly model, behavioral biometric profiling or autonomous risk decision.
- Video surveillance, camera integration, GPS tracking or device keystroke monitoring.
- Accounting/legal conclusion, fraud adjudication or evidence export for court without a separate policy.
- Raw request/response archival, raw JWT/PIN/payment credentials or customer phone in Audit.
- Replacing Reporting dashboard, Order/Payment/Shift source of truth or Identity authorization.

## 8. Design principles

1. **Source service owns state:** Audit records facts about a committed operation; it never becomes the mutation authority.
2. **Append-only evidence:** Historical events are immutable; corrections are new events, never in-place edits.
3. **Server-derived scope:** Client filters are requests only; Identity and repository predicates enforce tenant/branch access.
4. **Minimum necessary data:** Store references and safe snapshots, not raw payloads or sensitive credentials.
5. **Explainable detection:** P2 rules and baselines must identify the evidence and version used for a finding.
6. **At-least-once safe:** Outbox, inbox dedupe, retries, DLQ and replay are part of correctness, not optional infrastructure.
7. **No silent gaps:** Missing source/projection data is visible as `PARTIAL`/`STALE`/`UNAVAILABLE` and raises operational telemetry.
8. **Human-in-the-loop:** Anomaly findings support investigation; only authorized humans can resolve cases.

## 9. Known risks and mitigations

| Risk/unknown | Impact | Initial mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| A source service omits an audit event | Blind spot in forensic timeline | Parent event amendments, outbox reconciliation and source-vs-Audit completeness metrics |
| Duplicate/out-of-order delivery | Double evidence or wrong sequence | Event ID/source version dedupe, monotonic watermark and replay fixtures |
| Clock skew or timezone ambiguity | Wrong shift/time-window baseline | UTC event time + branch-local business time + server-resolved timezone policy |
| Audit data leaks PII/secrets | Privacy/security incident | Allowlisted schemas, redaction at producer/consumer, log/trace scanning and no raw payload storage |
| False positive anomaly | Manager fatigue or unfair action | Explainable rules, severity thresholds, baseline warm-up, dismiss reason and no auto-blocking |
| High event volume over 7 years | Query/retention cost and latency | Time-based partition/archive policy, query-first indexes, bounded filters and measured thresholds |
| Unauthorized case/evidence reference | Cross-branch information leak | Re-check scope on case and evidence fetch, opaque IDs, no existence leakage and cache isolation |
| Audit store unavailable | Missing immediate visibility | Durable producer outbox, queue/DLQ, lag state and restricted replay; source mutation does not wait on Audit query path |

## 10. Open decisions for Requirements

1. Exact action taxonomy and event names for cancel, discount/override, drawer open, access denial and case transitions.
2. Whether drawer-open event is emitted by POS Device, Shift or Payment boundary and how manual/automatic triggers are distinguished.
3. Discount policy fields: manual override, voucher/promotion application, approval threshold and before/after allocation.
4. Integrity mechanism: database append-only controls plus hash evidence, or a stronger chained/externally anchored audit digest.
5. Audit query freshness target, maximum range, pagination/export rules and search dimensions.
6. Baseline warm-up period, anomaly rule catalog, thresholds, severity and minimum sample size.
7. Finding/case ownership, notification laterality and retention of dismissed/false-positive cases.
8. Legal hold/archive/delete behavior within seven-year minimum retention.
9. Audit permissions and restricted operations permission names in Identity.
